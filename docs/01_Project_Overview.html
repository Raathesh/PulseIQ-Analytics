Core value

A founder should not need four reports to know how the business is doing. PulseIQ builds a trusted warehouse, clear metrics and dashboards that connect headcount, pay, cost, cash and productivity in one place, with privacy built in.

What you will build

You will build a star-schema analytics warehouse with data-quality gates, a governed metric layer, dashboards for people, finance and teams, an executive company-health scorecard, automated reports and a hosted self-service experience. Your numbers are validated against answer keys inside the dataset.

Real-world scenario: Zenith Nova's leadership asks why payroll cost moved, which department is overspending its budget, where cash is stuck in unpaid invoices and which teams are overloaded. PulseIQ answers each in a dashboard and reconciles to the paisa.

Your place in Aura Workforce

Aura Workforce unifies people, payroll, accounts and teams behind one login, made intelligent by AI. Six tracks build it as one product for one fictional client, Zenith Nova Technologies. Every track ships on its own: you never wait for, or depend on, another track. You are welcome to read the other five task files to see the full picture.

Go-live standard

Deployment is mandatory. By the end of Phase 4 your PulseIQ dashboards must be live online with a shareable link, loading from a hosted warehouse, refreshing on schedule and opening on a phone. Salary-level data must be protected, and small groups must be hidden.

Final submission

  • Live dashboard link and a screenshot-rich walkthrough
  • SQL and pipeline code in GitHub with a clear README and warehouse diagram
  • Reconciliation proof against the answer keys and a data-quality report
  • Metric and lineage documentation
  • A board-style insight deck and a 5-minute demo video

Work like a real company

  • Treat every task as a ticket: read the goal, write your own acceptance checks, then deliver the output named in the task.
  • Use Git from day one: small commits, one branch per task, a pull request with a clear description, and a short daily log.
  • Everything runs on your own, fully fictional Zenith Nova data. No other track is needed to finish yours.
  • At the end of each week, record a 2-minute demo of what you shipped. Your Week 4 demo is your final product walkthrough.
